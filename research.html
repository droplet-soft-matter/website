What we study

Research

Physics of droplets, wetting and adhesion science, and soft matter — from bioinspired surfaces to droplet electrification. We combine theory, simulation, and experiment to uncover and rationalize new physical phenomena.

Ongoing

Wetting & Adhesion

How droplets stick, slide, and let go. We study the forces governing contact lines, capillarity, and adhesion between liquids and solids.
Ongoing

Active matter

Active matter is ubiquitous in nature, from bacterial swarms to bird flocks. We show that evaporating condensate droplets form a synthetic active-matter system with applications in heat transfer and water harvesting.
Ongoing

Coulomb explosions

During evaporation, charged millimetric water drops can spontaneously emit fine liquid jets which break up into microdroplets, a phenomenon known as Coulomb explosion.

Ongoing

Droplet electrification

When water rubs against a solid surface, it naturally acquires electric charges. We study the consequences of this spontaneous droplet electrification.
Past

Speech droplets

When we speak, we expel a spray of respiratory droplets into the air, which can carry pathogens over meters. Here, we show that face masks substantially reduce speech droplets.
Past

Fluid jet

Fluid jet impact dynamics on soft materials. We found that jet rebound is unaffected by wetting properties.
